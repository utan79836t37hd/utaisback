utan343

A posting assistant for the art channel “一興でござる” · version of 10 November 2026

utan343 is used by the operator of one YouTube channel about Japanese art. When the operator finishes a short video, utan343 places it on that channel with the words the operator prepared. It is a private tool, not a public service.

In practice

Finished videos sit in a folder on the operator's computer. Each has a small text file with its title, description and hashtags. Once a day utan343 takes the next video, uploads it to the operator's channel and schedules its publication. It also looks at the public view, like and comment counts of recent uploads so the operator can follow results.

Never

utan343 does not alter channel settings, touch monetization, comment, message anyone, or pass data to others.
